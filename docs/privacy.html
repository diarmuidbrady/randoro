Privacy Policy

Last updated 16 August 2026

randoro does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

No data collected

The app does not require an account. It does not use analytics or tracking, and it does not send any information from your device to us or to any third party. Any settings you configure, such as your rounds and durations, stay on your device.

On-device features

randoro plays audio and, if you enable them, uses screen flash and vibration during a workout. These run entirely on your device.
