Train your reactions solo with Randoro. It fires random beeps that force you to react and stay alert throughout the round.

Randoro keeps you accountable when you train alone. Set your rounds and start, and the beeps come at random intervals within the bounds you choose.

The cues play through your phone, headphones, or a speaker, and keep going in the background and on the lock screen so you can pocket the phone mid-round. They also play alongside Spotify, so you can train to your own music.

What it does

  • Random cues. Beeps fire at random intervals during work rounds, within the intensity you choose.
  • Full round structure. Warm-up, rounds, work and rest durations, and cool-down.
  • Flash and vibration. Optional screen flash and vibration on each cue.
  • Background audio. Cues keep playing on the lock screen and behind other apps.
