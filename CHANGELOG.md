# Changelog

What changed for users in each release, newest first.

## 1.1.0 — 25 Oct 2026

App Store update, and the first Android release (closed testing).

### Added
- Three workout styles. **Preset**: Intensity and Attack Length. **Manual**: set the avg. anticipation time before each beep (or the attacks per round) and a fixed execution time, with the avg. time between beeps shown. **Simple**: a plain interval timer with no random beeps.
- A 10-second warning and a 3-2-1 countdown before each round.
- A beep count during work rounds, e.g. "2 of 3 beeps".
- A "How it works" guide with sound demos. It opens on first launch and from Setup.
- A heartbeat sound and a new heartbeat-trace animation on the home screen.

### Changed
- Dark launch screen.
- Opening Randoro no longer pauses your music.
- Asks for fewer permissions: no microphone, overlay or storage access.
- GET READY only responds once it has appeared.

### Fixed
- The first beep is no longer cut off on Bluetooth speakers.
- The warm-up sound no longer plays twice when you press BEGIN.
- Android: beeps keep playing in the background and on the lock screen, the first play of each beep is heard, and the app icon is no longer cropped.

## 1.0.1 — 19 Sep 2026

App Store only.

### Changed
- Intensity presets leave longer waits between beeps: Low 9 s, Medium 6 s, High 3 s (were 5, 3 and 1 s).
- Bigger combinations get more time to attack: 3-4 punches 2 s, 5-6+ punches 3 s (were 1.5 and 2 s).

## 1.0.0 — 16 Aug 2026

First App Store release.

- Random beeps during work rounds, so you react to the unpredictable.
- Set the warm-up, number of rounds, work and rest durations, and cool-down; swap whether work or rest comes first.
- Intensity (Low, Medium, High) sets how often beeps fire; Combo Count (1-2, 3-4, 5-6+) sets the time you get after each beep.
- Optional screen flash and vibration on each beep.
- Jump between phases, pause and resume.
- Beeps keep playing in the background, on the lock screen, over your music and through Bluetooth.
- Animated home screen, and support for larger text sizes.
