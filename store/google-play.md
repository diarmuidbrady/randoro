## Full description (4,000)

The same as the App Store description in `app-store.md`; keep the two in step.

```
Randoro is a reaction training timer for boxing. It fires random beeps during your workout, so you react to the cue instead of going through the motions.

Set your warm-up, rounds, work and rest times, and cool-down, then choose a workout style:
• Preset: pick how often the beeps come and how many punches you throw after each beep.
• Manual: set the timing yourself: the average wait before each beep (or the number of attacks per round), plus how long you get to attack.
• Simple: a plain interval timer with no random beeps.

While you train
• A large countdown with your elapsed and remaining time
• A 10-second warning and a 3-2-1 countdown into each round
• A beep count, e.g. "2 of 3 beeps"
• Screen flash and vibration on each beep, on or off
• Jump between phases, pause, and resume

Audio keeps playing in the background and on the lock screen, through your phone, headphones or speaker, and over your music.

New to Randoro? The How it works guide walks you through it, with sound demos.

Randoro is built for solo training, whether you are on the bag, shadow boxing, or keeping fit. Set your rounds, hit start, and react.
```
